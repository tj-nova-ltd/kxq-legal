KXQ · Privacy

Privacy Policy

What KXQ collects, why, where it goes and how to erase it.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

01

Who we are

KXQ — Pack Rip Club ("KXQ", "we") is an iPhone app for opening digital packs of collectible cards and toys. The data controller is TJ NOVA LTD, 124-128 City Road, London EC1V 2NX, United Kingdom. Questions: info@tjnovaltd.com.

02

Preview status

KXQ is currently in preview. Pack purchases go through a demonstration Apple Pay flow and nobody is charged, pack results are scripted demonstrations, the wallet shown in the app uses the Solana devnet test network, and no physical items are shipped yet. We will update this page before any of that changes.

03

Account data

You can browse without an account. If you sign in with Apple, Google or email, we keep: your email address (or Apple's private relay address), the name you choose to share, an account ID, the identifier Apple or Google returns for your sign-in, a salted hash of your password if you set one (never the password itself), your language, and your active sessions (device type, approximate IP address and expiry). Sessions expire after 7 days of inactivity.

Accounts are run on TJ NOVA LTD's own account service (TJBase), hosted on Cloudflare. One-time sign-in codes are sent by email through our mail provider and expire after 10 minutes.

04

Packs, collection and marketplace

Your pack history, revealed items, vault contents, watch list and any marketplace listings you create are stored on your iPhone in this preview. When syncing is switched on in a later version they will be linked to your account so you can restore them on a new device.

If physical redemption opens, we will ask for a shipping name, address and phone number only when you request a shipment, and use them only to deliver that item.

05

Wallet and blockchain

KXQ shows a Solana wallet address for your collection. Public addresses and on-chain records are public by design and cannot be changed or deleted by us or anyone else. To show a balance, the app sends that public address to a public Solana RPC endpoint (api.devnet.solana.com during preview). KXQ never asks for, stores or sends a seed phrase or private key.

06

Payments

Payments in KXQ use Apple Pay or the App Store. Card details are handled by Apple and your bank; we never see your full card number. During preview the Apple Pay sheet is a demonstration and no money moves.

07

AI price notes

Price figures in KXQ are calculated on your device from demonstration market data. An optional AI price note is not switched on in the current version. If we enable it, it will use Cloudflare Workers AI (Cloudflare, Inc.), it will receive only the item's name, set and grade — never your account details — and under Cloudflare's Workers AI terms the input and output are not kept or used to train models. Estimates are not financial advice.

08

What we do not do

No advertising SDKs, no cross-app tracking, no third-party analytics, no sale or sharing of personal data. Live Activities, widgets and haptics run on your device.

09

Security records

Our servers on Cloudflare see IP addresses and request metadata to deliver the service and block abuse. The account service keeps a security log of account events (time, action, account ID and IP address). These records are kept only as long as needed for security, fraud prevention and legal obligations.

10

Legal bases and retention

We use account data to provide the service you ask for (contract), and security logs for our legitimate interest in keeping KXQ safe. Account data is kept until you delete your account. On deletion we erase your email address, name, password hash, sign-in identifiers and sessions at once; what remains is an anonymous account ID and the security log described above. Records we must keep by law (for example transaction records once real payments exist) are kept only for the required period.

11

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or object to processing. Email info@tjnovaltd.com from your account email. You may complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

12

Age

KXQ is for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18; if you believe a minor has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

13

International transfers

Cloudflare processes data on its global network. Where data leaves the UK we rely on the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or an adequacy decision.

14

Changes

We will post changes here and update the date above. Material changes will also be shown in the app.

Delete your account

How to permanently remove your KXQ account and data.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

01

In the app

Open KXQ › Profile › Account › Delete account and confirm. Your account, sessions and the data linked to it are removed straight away and you are signed out on every device.

02

By email

If you cannot open the app, email info@tjnovaltd.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my KXQ account". We confirm and complete deletion within 30 days.

03

What is deleted

Your email address, name, sign-in identifiers, password hash, sessions, and any synced pack history, vault, watch list and listings. Data stored only on your iPhone is removed when you delete the app. An anonymous account ID and the security log of account events (time, action, IP address) are kept for fraud prevention, as explained in the Privacy Policy.

04

What cannot be deleted

Public blockchain records (for example a wallet address or an ownership record already written on-chain) cannot be changed by anyone. Records we must keep by law, such as transaction records once real payments exist, are kept only for the legally required period.

05

Sign in with Apple

If you used Sign in with Apple, we also revoke KXQ's access with Apple when you delete your account. You can review it in iPhone Settings › your name › Sign in with Apple.

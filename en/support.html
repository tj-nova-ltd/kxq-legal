Support

Stuck on a rip? We answer every message.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

01

Contact

Email info@tjnovaltd.com with your account email, device model and iOS version. We reply within 2 business days (UK time).

02

Sign-in

You can sign in with Apple, Google or email. If an email code does not arrive within a few minutes, check spam and request a new code; each code lasts 10 minutes.

03

Purchases

During preview, Apple Pay is a demonstration and you are never charged. For App Store charges in a future version, refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

04

Packs, vault and marketplace

Pack odds are shown on each pack before you open it. Revealed items appear in your Vault. Preview items have no cash value and cannot be redeemed yet.

05

Wallet

The wallet shown in KXQ uses the Solana devnet test network in preview. We will never ask for a seed phrase or private key — anyone who does is not us.

06

Language

KXQ follows your iPhone's language. To change it, open Settings › Apps › KXQ › Language on your iPhone.

07

Delete your data

See the Delete account page for in-app and email options.
